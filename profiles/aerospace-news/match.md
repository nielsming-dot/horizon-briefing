# Aerospace news profile

Use this profile for spaceflight and aerospace developments: launches and mission operations, new vehicles and engines, commercial space companies and contracts, government space programs and budgets, satellites and constellations, space stations and human spaceflight, and defense-space activities.

Typical items include launch outcomes and anomalies, vehicle development milestones, major contract and policy decisions, station crew rotations, and constellation deployments.

Do not route the scientific results of space missions here; route those to the astronomy profile. Route satellite-imagery or space-policy stories whose substance is earth science to the profile that matches the science.
