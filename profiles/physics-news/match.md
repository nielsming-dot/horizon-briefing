# Physics news profile

Use this profile for developments in physics research: condensed matter and quantum materials, particle and nuclear physics, quantum information and computing fundamentals, atomic/molecular/optical physics, plasma and fusion science, gravitational physics, and major instruments or facilities built for physics.

Typical items include significant experimental results, precision measurements that test or constrain theory, new theoretical results with testable consequences, and facility milestones such as colliders, detectors, or quantum processors reaching design performance.

Do not route astronomical observations and celestial discoveries here; route those to the astronomy profile. Route commercial quantum-computing or chip product announcements to the technology or AI profile when the news is about the product rather than the physics.
