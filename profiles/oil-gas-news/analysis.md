# Evaluation goal

Evaluate whether oil and gas industry news gives readers timely, concrete, and decision-relevant information about projects, companies, markets, policy, or technology in the petroleum value chain.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Industry-shaping.** Decisions or events that move supply at scale (OPEC+ quota changes with numbers, major FIDs or startup of large fields/LNG trains), NOC/supermajors M&A above tens of billions, or policy changes that restructure production, trade, or pricing.
- **7-8: Important.** Significant project milestones, discoveries with stated volumes and economics, consequential strategy or leadership changes at major operators, meaningful regulatory shifts (pricing mechanisms, quotas, pipelines, carbon rules), and field-proven technology results with measured gains.
- **5-6: Useful.** Routine project progress, moderate-sized contracts and licenses, secondary-market commentary grounded in data, and incremental technology adoption stories.
- **3-4: Low value.** Price moves without cause or persistence, speculative deal rumors, press-release partnerships without committed capital, or commentary that repeats known facts.
- **0-2: Noise.** Rumors without sourcing, promotional content, or material with no oil and gas substance.

# Evaluation guidance

Reward specificity: named projects, volumes (bbl/d, bcm, tpa), contract values, quota numbers, dates, and decision bodies. Distinguish final investment decisions from plans and studies, and startup from mechanical completion. For Chinese sources, note relevance to domestic policy or NOC operations explicitly. Do not reward sweeping "energy transition" framing without concrete oil and gas consequences.
