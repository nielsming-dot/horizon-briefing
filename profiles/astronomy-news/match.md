# Astronomy news profile

Use this profile for discoveries and science about the universe beyond Earth: exoplanets, stars and stellar evolution, black holes and compact objects, galaxies and cosmology, the interstellar medium, solar-system science and planetary bodies, solar and space weather events, sky-watching events, and astronomical instruments such as telescopes and observatories (science-driven news).

Typical items include new observations or catalogs, mission science results from space telescopes and probes, notable sky events visible to observers, and theoretical or simulation results with observational consequences.

Do not route launch, mission operations, or space-industry business news here; route those to the aerospace profile. Route planetary-mission engineering milestones to aerospace when the news is the operation rather than the science result.
