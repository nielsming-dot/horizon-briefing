# Chemistry and chemical industry news profile

Use this profile for chemistry and chemical-engineering developments: catalysis, synthesis methods, materials and polymers, electrochemistry at the materials level, industrial processes and process intensification, chemical plant news, feedstocks, and chemical-industry markets and safety.

Typical items include new catalysts or routes with measured performance, scale-up and commercialization milestones, plant investments or incidents, regulatory changes for chemicals, and analytical-technique advances.

Do not route pharmaceutical clinical results here; route those to the medicine profile. Route battery, solar, and hydrogen technology whose point is the energy application to the energy profile when the chemistry is incidental.
