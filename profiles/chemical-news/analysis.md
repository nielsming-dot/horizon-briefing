# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the importance of chemistry and chemical-industry developments for researchers and industrial practitioners.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Field-changing.** Synthesis or catalysis results that remove major cost, safety, or scale barriers; first commercial routes to important compounds; or incidents/regulation with industry-wide consequences.
- **7-8: Significant.** Measured improvements in yield, selectivity, durability, or cost at relevant scales; notable scale-up milestones; major plant decisions; consequential chemical regulation.
- **5-6: Interesting.** Laboratory-scale advances with plausible paths, incremental process improvements, or market moves affecting specific product lines.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Routine portfolio announcements, unquantified claims of "greener" processes, or lab results far from any scale context.
- **0-2: Noise.** Promotional material, unverified claims, or off-topic content.

# Evaluation guidance

Reward quantified performance with baselines (yield, selectivity, stability, cost per unit) over adjectives, and note the scale at which results were obtained. Distinguish demonstrated performance from projected economics. Treat safety and environmental incidents in proportion to verified consequence, not initial speculation.
