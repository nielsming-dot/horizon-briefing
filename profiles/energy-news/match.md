# Energy news profile

Use this profile for developments in energy: solar, wind, storage, grids and transmission, nuclear fission and fusion programs, hydrogen and fuels, electric-vehicle charging infrastructure, energy markets and prices, utility strategy, and energy-climate policy.

Typical items include deployment and cost milestones, technology results at pilot or commercial scale, major project approvals and cancellations, market and price events, and consequential regulation or subsidies.

Do not route basic materials research whose relevance is the material itself to this profile; route it to the chemistry profile. Route climate science findings without an energy-system angle elsewhere, and vehicle product news without charging or grid substance to the technology profile.
