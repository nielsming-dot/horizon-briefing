# Evaluation goal

Evaluate whether energy news gives readers timely, quantified, and consequential information about energy technology, markets, projects, or policy.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: System-shifting.** Cost or deployment results that change build-out economics, first-of-kind commercial projects succeeding, major transmission or nuclear program decisions, or policy with measurable supply consequences.
- **7-8: Important.** Notable project milestones, credible cost records, significant market events, storage or grid advances at relevant scale, and consequential regulation.
- **5-6: Useful.** Pilot results with clear context, incremental deployment news, routine market commentary, or state-level policy steps.
- **3-4: Low value.** Project rumors, funding rounds without technical substance, unquantified "record-breaking" claims, or advocacy pieces without new facts.
- **0-2: Noise.** Speculation, promotional content, or material with no energy substance.

# Evaluation guidance

Weight quantified figures with baselines: cost per unit (LCOE, $/kWh, $/kW), capacity and duration, utilization, and delivery dates. Distinguish commissioned projects from announced ones, and awarded capacity from built capacity. Treat price moves in proportion to cause and persistence, not headlines.
