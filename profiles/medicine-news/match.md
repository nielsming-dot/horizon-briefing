# Medical news profile

Use this profile for developments in medicine and public health: clinical trial results, drug and device approvals, guideline changes, epidemiological findings, outbreaks and health-security events, health-system and coverage policy, and clinically meaningful research on disease mechanisms or treatment.

Typical items include phase II/III trial readouts, regulatory decisions by agencies such as the FDA or EMA, landmark observational studies, screening and diagnostic advances, and major guideline revisions.

Do not route basic biochemistry or materials research with no near-term clinical relevance here; route those to the chemistry profile. Route hospital-industry M&A and health-insurer earnings without a clinical or policy angle to the technology or finance profile only if no better fit exists.
