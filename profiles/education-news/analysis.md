# Evaluation goal

Evaluate whether education news gives readers timely, credible, and consequential information about teaching, learning, institutions, or education policy.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Far-reaching.** Decisions or findings that change practice or access at national or multi-state scale, landmark litigation, or rigorous studies overturning prevailing practice.
- **7-8: Important.** Notable policy changes with defined scope, well-designed studies with actionable outcomes, major enrollment or financial shifts at significant institutions, and consequential edtech evidence.
- **5-6: Useful.** State or district-level developments, pilot results with clear limitations, adoption news with usage evidence, or moderate funding changes.
- **3-4: Low value.** Anecdote-driven coverage, opinion without new facts, routine personnel changes, or product announcements without adoption or outcome evidence.
- **0-2: Noise.** Promotional content, moral-panic coverage without evidence, or off-topic material.

# Evaluation guidance

Prefer items with defined populations, measurable outcomes, and stated scope over framing and rhetoric. Distinguish rigorous research (randomized or well-controlled, preregistered) from surveys and testimonials. Note who is affected and at what scale, and treat announced pilots as less important than completed ones.
