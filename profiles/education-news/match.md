# Education news profile

Use this profile for developments in education: K-12 and higher-education policy and funding, teaching and learning research, education technology products with evidence of use or outcomes, admissions and access, teacher workforce, and the education industry.

Typical items include policy and curriculum changes with stated scope, studies with measurable learning outcomes, edtech adoption or efficacy findings, enrollment and financial shifts at institutions, and court or legislative decisions affecting schools and universities.

Do not route AI model research here; route it to the AI profile even when education is a stated application. Route general consumer software and hardware without an education-specific substance to the technology profile.
