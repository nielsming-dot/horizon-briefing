# Literature news profile

Use this profile for the world of books and writing: notable new fiction, poetry, essays, and literary criticism; author news; major prizes and awards; publishing-industry developments; translations and world literature; archives and literary-history findings; and festivals or institutional changes in the literary world.

Typical items include award announcements, significant publications and serializations, publishing mergers or imprint changes with reader-facing consequences, posthumous or archival releases, and substantive criticism that reframes a writer or movement.

Do not route generic "best books to read" listicles or affiliate-driven roundups here unless they carry real critical judgment. Route film/TV adaptations without a literary-world angle to the technology profile only if no better fit exists.
