# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the importance of timely artificial intelligence news for readers who build, research, or deploy AI systems.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Groundbreaking.** Frontier-capability jumps, major open-weight releases, results that change a field's consensus, or regulation that materially reshapes what can be built or deployed.
- **7-8: High value.** Notable model releases, meaningful benchmark or evaluation advances, important open-source tools, credible safety or incident findings, and consequential policy moves.
- **5-6: Interesting.** Incremental model updates, useful engineering techniques, moderate community debates, or vendor announcements with substance but limited reach.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Routine fine-tunes, benchmark gaming without methodological novelty, hype-driven coverage, or product updates without technical substance.
- **0-2: Noise.** Speculation without evidence, promotional content, fabricated or unverifiable claims, or off-topic material.

# Evaluation guidance

Consider the strength of evidence (paper with method and data beats press release), the measurability of the claimed advance, breadth of affected users, and reproducibility. Reward concrete benchmark comparisons with baselines; discount headline numbers without test conditions. Treat genuine independent evaluations and reproductions as strong evidence, and treat follower counts or stock moves as no evidence of technical importance.
