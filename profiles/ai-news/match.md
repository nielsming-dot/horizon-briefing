# AI news profile

Use this profile for timely developments in artificial intelligence and machine learning: new models and model releases, benchmark results, research breakthroughs, agent and tooling ecosystems, AI infrastructure (chips, training and inference systems), safety and alignment findings, and AI regulation with direct technical consequences.

Typical items include model or product launches, notable papers with measurable advances, open-source releases, capability or safety evaluations, dataset and training disclosures, and AI policy changes that affect what practitioners may build or deploy.

Do not route general software engineering, consumer hardware, or company earnings without an AI-specific angle here; route those to the technology news profile. Route AI-era human-interest commentary and pure opinion pieces without new facts to a lower score under this profile only if no better profile fits.
