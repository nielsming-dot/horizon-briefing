# Chemical products and polymer markets profile

Use this profile for the commodity chemicals and polymers market: olefins and aromatics chains (ethylene, propylene, PX-PTA-MEG), polyolefins (PP, PE including LLDPE/LDPE/HDPE), PVC, PS, ABS, PET and polyester, engineering plastics, synthetic rubber, methanol and coal-to-chemicals (CTO/MTO), fertilizers, and specialty chemical products when the news is about markets or capacity.

Typical items include price movements with stated drivers, margin and spread changes, capacity additions and startups with volumes, plant shutdowns and turnarounds with capacity impact, investment decisions for new plants, import/export volumes tariffs and trade measures, feedstock cost shifts (naphtha, propane, coal) affecting polymer economics, and demand shifts in downstream sectors (packaging, construction, automotive, agriculture).

Do not route laboratory chemistry research, catalysis science without a market or capacity context here; route those to the chemistry profile. Route upstream oil and gas exploration, production, and OPEC+ policy to the oil and gas profile. Route refining crude throughput news without a products angle to oil and gas; route petrochemical integration project news (refining-petrochemical complexes) here when the substance is chemical products capacity.
