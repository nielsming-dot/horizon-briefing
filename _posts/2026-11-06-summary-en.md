### [Baidu Open-Sources ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base, a 280B MoE Model](https://www.qbitai.com/2026/11/507787.html) ⭐️ 7.0/10

On November 5, 2026, Baidu open-sourced ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base \(文心5.5 Flash Base\), a mixture-of-experts base model with 280 billion total parameters and 14 billion activated per token, pretrained on 33.5 trillion tokens with a 128K pretraining context and up to 1M-token context in fine-tuning and inference. Baidu claims same-size SOTA results across knowledge, reasoning, math, code, multilingual, and long-context benchmarks, reportedly surpassing DeepSeek-V4-Flash-Base, Hy3-preview-Base, and MiMo-V2.5-Base; these comparisons are vendor self-reported and have not been independently reproduced. Architectural changes include Latent-Gated Sparse Attention \(combining hybrid sparse attention, low-rank gated attention with 75% fewer gating projection parameters, and virtual head attention\), a Multi-Router Latent MoE design, and an ECHO training objective that reuses the backbone&\#x27;s final layer instead of a separate multi-token prediction module. The weights are available on Hugging Face at baidu/ERNIE-5.5-Flash-Base.

rss · 量子位 · Nov 5, 07:15

**「Background」** ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base follows Baidu&\#x27;s earlier ERNIE-series base models and builds on the MoE approach used in recent large open models, where a large total parameter count is kept affordable by activating only a small subset per token. The release also positions itself against contemporaries such as DeepSeek-V4-Flash-Base, Hy3-preview-Base, and MiMo-V2.5-Base, reflecting a trend of Chinese labs competing on open-weight base models tuned for efficiency as long-context and agent workloads raise compute and memory costs.

**「Why it matters for developers」** As a base model rather than a chat-tuned release, ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base is positioned for fine-tuning and domain customization, and the reused ECHO layer doubles as a speculative-decoding draft model, which could reduce inference cost for teams deploying the model as-is. However, since the SOTA claims come from Baidu&\#x27;s own benchmarks, teams evaluating it for production should run their own comparisons against competing open-weight models before committing.

### [Meta-Analysis: Low-Grade Proteinuria Signals Kidney Risk in IgA Nephropathy](https://www.medpagetoday.com/spotlight/igan/123728) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A systematic review and meta-analysis published in the Clinical Journal of the American Society of Nephrology found that even low-grade proteinuria \(0.5 to 1.0 g/day\) significantly increases the risk of adverse kidney outcomes in IgA nephropathy. The analysis pooled 23 studies of 15,289 patients across Asia, Europe, and North America, with 3.1 to more than 9 years of follow-up. Compared with proteinuria below 0.5 g/day, baseline low-grade proteinuria was associated with a 73% higher risk of adverse outcomes \(pooled HR 1.73, 95% CI 1.36-2.20\), while time-averaged low-grade proteinuria carried nearly triple the risk \(pooled HR 2.87, 95% CI 1.48-5.56\), plus faster annual eGFR loss \(-1.02 mL/min/1.73 m² per year\). The findings reinforce the 2025 KDIGO guideline change lowering the proteinuria target from &lt;1.0 to &lt;0.5 g/day, though the evidence is observational and does not prove that treating to this target improves outcomes.

rss · MedPage Today · Nov 5, 17:45

**「Why this matters」** IgA nephropathy is the most common primary glomerulonephritis worldwide, and up to 50% of affected patients progress to kidney failure within 10 to 20 years. Historically, proteinuria below 1.0 g/day was considered relatively low risk and shaped both clinical decision making and trial design, which typically enrolled only patients above that threshold. The 2025 KDIGO guideline update lowered the proteinuria treatment target from less than 1.0 g/day to less than 0.5 g/day, but this recommendation rested largely on individual observational studies rather than a systematic review, a gap this meta-analysis aimed to address.

**「Practice Implications」** For clinicians, the results argue against dismissing proteinuria of 0.5 to 1.0 g/day as reassuring in IgA nephropathy and support pursuing sustained suppression below 0.5 g/day. A practical barrier remains: recent clinical trials almost exclusively enrolled patients with proteinuria above 1.0 g/day, so patients in the 0.5 to 1.0 g/day range may be excluded from newer disease-modifying therapies, since guideline indications and regulatory approvals often track trial inclusion criteria. The authors and editorialists call for interventional trials that include patients with low-grade proteinuria.

The two trace how demonstrations that began at the start of the academic year over the state of education grew into broader agitation alongside trade unions over socioeconomic conditions. They discuss the structure of the French education system, crumbling school buildings in the banlieues, the contested Parcoursup admissions portal introduced in 2018, and the police&\#x27;s role in escalating violence. The conversation also touches on property damage narratives versus harm to people and the protests&\#x27; possible effect on spring elections.

**「The Writers」** ZZ Packer, author of the story collection Drinking Coffee Elsewhere, was on a brief stint at the American University of Paris, while Nafkote Tamirat, author of The Parking Lot Attendant and the forthcoming Teret Teret, is affiliated with the university and lives in France full-time. Their shared connection to the university and Tamirat&\#x27;s residency gave the episode first-hand grounding in the unrest, which according to a linked New York Times report drew hundreds of thousands of protesters in another round in late October 2026.
